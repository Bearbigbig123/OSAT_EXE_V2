Cpk By Site demo dataset
========================
Charts: 10
Date range: 2026-08-01 through 2026-10-31 (92 daily rows per chart)
Input mode: choose 'By Site' on the Cpk page
Site columns: site1...siteN (no underscore)
point_val: mean of all available site readings at that timestamp
Cpk By Site: all non-empty site cells in the selected period are pooled
